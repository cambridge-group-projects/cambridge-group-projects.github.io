# Key dates

The full schedule for the **2026–27** cycle, from the briefing lecture in
Michaelmas to the exhibition and awards on the last Wednesday of Lent.

!!! warning "Check these against the departmental pages"

    Dates marked **confirmed** are fixed. The rest are projected from the
    University's official Term and Full Term dates using the same week-offsets as
    the published 2025–26 schedule — check them against the departmental
    [Important dates](https://www.cst.cam.ac.uk/teaching/part-ib/group-projects/important-dates)
    page and Moodle once published.

## Things that are easy to get wrong

<div class="grid cards" markdown>

-   :material-calendar-account: **Client meetings are scheduled by your group**

    ---

    Client meetings can be online or in person. Each group is responsible for
    scheduling its meetings within the specified week. Clients — not the groups —
    may propose more frequent short meetings, but there must be at least the four
    meetings specified. The timing and format of the final exhibition are to be
    confirmed.

-   :material-calendar-clock: **Reserved slots, Tue & Thu**

    ---

    The lecture timetable reserves 11:00–13:00 on every Tuesday and Thursday of Lent
    term. These are to give explicit time for groups to meet in order to conduct
    their internal co-ordination.

-   :material-clock-alert: **The 12-noon rule**

    ---

    Every formal document is delivered as a PDF to the client before 12 noon on the
    day *preceding* the meeting — not on the morning of it — so the client has time
    to read it properly.

</div>
