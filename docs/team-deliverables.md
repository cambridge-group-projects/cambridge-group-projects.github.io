# Deliverables

Every deliverable, team action and individual task across the seven phases —
seventeen items in total. **D** marks a document the client receives, **T** a team
action, and **I** something every student does individually.

## Phase I · Project selection

- [ ] **I** — Record topic preferences via Moodle — *Mon 30 Nov 2026*

## Phase II · Kickoff and risk analysis

- [ ] **T** — Contact person introduces themselves to the administration team — *Week 1*
- [ ] **T** — Schedule and hold Client Meeting 1 — *Week 1*
- [ ] **D** — Risk assessment (max 50 words) to the group project email — *within 24 hours of Meeting 1*

## Phase III · Requirements analysis

- [ ] **T** — Schedule Client Meeting 2 — *Week 2*
- [ ] **D** — Draft Functional Specification & Project Plan to the client — *≥24 hours before Meeting 2*
- [ ] **D** — Final Functional Specification & Project Plan to client + project email — *within 24 hours of Meeting 2*

## Phase IV · Implementation

- [ ] **T** — Schedule Client Meeting 3 — *Week 4*
- [ ] **D** — Progress Report 1 (PDF) to client + project email — *≥24 hours before Meeting 3*
- [ ] **I** — Complete the interim assessment questionnaire on Moodle — *by Fri 19 Feb 2027, 12 noon*
- [ ] **T** — Schedule Client Meeting 4 — *Week 6*
- [ ] **D** — Progress Report 2 (PDF) to client + project email — *≥24 hours before Meeting 4*

## Phase V · Wrapup

- [ ] **D** — Video presentation (max 4 min) uploaded to Panopto via Moodle — *Thu 11 Mar 2027, 12 noon*
- [ ] **D** — Complete code repository (zip/tar) submitted via Moodle — *Thu 11 Mar 2027, 12 noon*
- [ ] **D** — Final Group Report (PDF) to client + project email — *by Wed 17 Mar 2027*

## Phase VI · Presentation and awards

- [ ] **I** — Review the videos, vote, and take part in the exhibition — *Week 8*

## Phase VII · Reflection and review

- [ ] **I** — Personal Report (PDF) submitted via Moodle — *Wed 17 Mar 2027, 5pm*

## How every deliverable must be sent

<div class="grid cards" markdown>

-   :material-file-pdf-box: **As a PDF**

    ---

    Every formal document is prepared as a PDF and sent to the client by email.

-   :material-clock-alert: **Before 12 noon the day before**

    ---

    Delivery must be made before 12 noon on the day preceding the meeting, so the
    client has time to review it.

-   :material-email: **Copied to the project email**

    ---

    The email containing the deliverable must be copied to the group project email
    address. These copies are used for assessment.

-   :material-format-title: **Team name in the subject line**

    ---

    The team name should appear in the subject line of each email, so the organisers
    can file it correctly.

</div>

!!! danger "The hard deadline: noon, Thursday of Week 8"

    At that time a full copy of your group code repository — as a zip or tar file —
    must be submitted to the examiners via Moodle, along with the video.

    **No credit will be given for features added after this.** All members must
    ensure the submitted repository contains the complete source code of the
    project, including their own individual contributions.
