
Client: Sashy Nathan, Commons Law CIC

Many people who appear in a magistrates' court carry problems alongside the
charge: nowhere safe to sleep, untreated mental illness, addiction, debt. These
issues are often also what bring them back to court, yet their solicitor has little
more than a web search to find help. Your task is to build Justice+, a tool that
lets a lawyer select a client's needs and a postcode and, in under three
minutes, receive a ranked shortlist of local services that are open, eligible and
genuinely taking referrals. The hard part is trust. Service directories go stale
fast, feeds from councils and charities overlap and contradict each other, and
one wrong fact sends a vulnerable person to a dead end. You'll need to design
a pipeline that merges messy sources, detects duplicates and changes, and
routes doubtful records for human checking, plus a tool that never
hallucinates or shows a fact it can't trace to a verified source. Security and
trust are paramount - nothing identifying the client may be entered or stored.
Your client co-founded an award-winning law firm and will provide seed data
from a pilot and practising lawyers to test with.
